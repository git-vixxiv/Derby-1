# Images

`splash.jpg` is the background art for the title screen: Joe's stadium scene
(night derby, pink #69 sedan), supplied 2026-11 as a 2400 x 1792, 3.2 MB
upload and saved here at 1920 x 1434, 453 KB. The full-size original is in
git history (commit bd0ea88, `images/Gemini_Generated_Image_hmsnbhmsnbhmsnbh.jpeg`).

To replace it, put a new file here with exactly the name `splash.jpg`; no
code change. `css/styles.css` positions it `center 25%` so the car sits below
the how-to-play panel on wide screens.

What the art should be:
- The scene only: stadium, mud, the car. **No text, buttons, panels or score
  plate painted into it.** The title, the menu panel and the how-to-play
  panel are real, working elements drawn on top, and they move with the
  screen size.
- Landscape, about 1920 px wide, JPG, under about 500 KB.
- Keep the car in the middle-to-right and lower half: the menu panel covers
  the left third and the title covers the top.
