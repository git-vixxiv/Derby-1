# Images

`splash.jpg` is the background art for the title screen. Put the file here
with exactly that name and the title screen picks it up; no code change.

What the art should be:
- The scene only: stadium, mud, the car. **No text, buttons, panels or score
  plate painted into it.** The title, the menu panel and the how-to-play
  panel are real, working elements drawn on top, and they move with the
  screen size.
- Landscape, about 1600 x 1200 px or larger, JPG, under about 500 KB.
- Keep the car in the middle-to-right and lower half: the menu panel covers
  the left third and the title covers the top.
